# Time waster simulator
It rhymes!
## Description
Basically designed to simulate your normal day if you are a doomscroller 
Also dont take this too seriously
I coded this with vibes only (not vibe coding though thats different)

## What to do
As a CRAP developer (code reviser and producer) this is my tutorial:
#### Scroll break!!!!
there is a tutorial on the site, but basically you live the life of bob, bob scrolls reels all day. Bob needs to stay productive or else he will be kicked out. Bob also has a limited amount of dopamine to waste, if bob loses all his dopamine he will forget to breathe.
Now when you become bob you should maximize hours wasted by scrolling, maintain productivity by coding stuff out of boredom, and end the day before bob forgets to breathe from a lack of dopamine.
